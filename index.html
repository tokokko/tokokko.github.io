Pocket Server AI

Privacy Policy

Overview

Pocket Server AI is designed to run entirely locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

No Data Collection

This application does not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Chat messages or prompts
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics
  • Location data

Local Processing

All AI processing happens locally on your device using on-device models. Your conversations never leave your device unless you explicitly export them.

Third-Party Services

Pocket Server AI does not integrate with any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Data Control

You have full control over all data created within the app. You may delete conversations at any time through the application interface.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in the future, updates will be included within the app. However, our core principle will remain the same: your data stays on your device.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer through the app’s support settings or distribution page.
